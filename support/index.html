Common questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap Restore purchases. You can also restore from the paywall.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Learn Python for AI. On Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

Do you provide API keys or AI credits?

No. Lessons show how to use your own accounts with AI providers, which are billed separately by those providers.

Where is my progress stored?

Only on your device. There is no account, so progress does not move to a new phone automatically.
