Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Learn Python for AI (“the app”) is published by Hussain Nasir Khan (“we”). Contact: hussainnasirkhan@icloud.com.

Summary

The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of ours. We do not collect your name, email, contacts or lesson activity. The free plan shows ads served by Google AdMob, and Pro removes them (see Advertising below). Purchases are processed by Apple or Google and our subscription provider, RevenueCat, as described below.

What stays on your device

Your lesson progress, quiz scores, bookmarks and appearance setting are stored only on your device, using the operating system’s standard app storage. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The app offers an optional “Pro” subscription and a one-time lifetime purchase. Payments are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.

To confirm what you have bought and to let you restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat (revenuecat.com). RevenueCat receives your purchase history and an anonymous app user ID generated by the service, together with basic device and app information (such as device type, OS version and country) and your IP address. This data is used only to verify entitlements and operate subscriptions. It is not linked to your name or email, and we do not use it for tracking or advertising. RevenueCat’s privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Advertising

The free edition shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To choose and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location and information about how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK) the app asks for your consent first, and you can change that choice under Ad privacy choices in the app. On iOS the app may ask permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites; if you decline, ads are not personalised. Buying Pro removes every ad, and the ad SDK is then no longer started. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We do not sell personal data.

Fonts

The app downloads its typefaces from Google Fonts the first time they are needed. This request exposes your IP address to Google, as any web request does. See Google’s privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Links to other sites

The Learning Resources screen and the paywall’s Terms and Privacy links open third-party pages (for example python.org and AI provider documentation) in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

What we do not do

No analytics, no crash-reporting service, and we do not sell personal data. The only advertising is the Google AdMob ads described above, shown on the free plan.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Your choices

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access or delete. To remove local data, delete the app. To cancel a subscription, use your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above.
